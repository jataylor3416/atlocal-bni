What changed

Polished still matters. Real wins trust.

Studio production

Polished, crafted, built to last.

Your brand film, website and grand opening. It builds credibility and gives you a library you own for years. We still love making it.

Unscripted

Raw, quick, straight to the phone.

Alexa, a Discover Strength trainer, talking about her favorite machine. No script, one take. It feels like a friend's recommendation, not an ad.

People now trust real over polished. Authenticity is king. For trackable leads and a real connection, creator content has taken over.

03
The avatar

People buy from people.

Your "avatar" is the face of your message: the person your customer already trusts. We cast two kinds.

Peer

Looks like your customer.

Someone in the same season of life, with the same problem, saying "this worked for me."

A busy mom who finally found 30 minutes for herself.

Credibility

Someone they already trust.

The expert, the coach, the local name with a following, explaining why it works.

A certified trainer walking you through the machine.
